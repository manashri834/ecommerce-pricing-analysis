# E-Commerce Pricing & Demand Analysis — Findings & Recommendations

## What I analyzed
Analyzed pricing, order volume, and review scores across 53 product categories 
using the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset (113,314 order-item records).

## Key findings
1. Categories like stationery, sports_leisure, health_beauty, toys, pet_shop, 
   perfumery, and housewares show strong order volume and solid review scores 
   (above 4.0) at relatively low average prices — a signal there's room to test 
   price increases without hurting demand.
2. Categories like construction_tools_safety, fixed_telephony, and 
   agro_industry_and_commerce show high average prices but noticeably lower 
   order volume (194-265 orders, versus 2,000-9,700+ for the top group) — 
   candidates for price cuts or bundled promotions.
3. Review scores across categories were fairly consistent (4.1 to 4.45), meaning 
   price and demand patterns matter more than review score differences when 
   deciding where to test pricing changes.

## Recommendation
Run a controlled price-increase test on 2-3 of the top underpriced categories 
(e.g. stationery, health_beauty), and test a promotional discount or bundling 
strategy on the identified overpriced, low-demand categories to lift order volume.
